About

J.K. Rowling had an idea that knocked her over once on a train from Manchester to London, and went on to create one of the most consequential fictional characters and worlds and north of a billion of dollars along the way.

I also had an idea: what if we had a Chrome browser that was built for the Violent Soho fan? A play on their famous song Covered in Chrome?

Well here it is. I await my billion dollars.

In all seriousness, I'm a longtime Violent Soho fan who had this idea a while ago, and have finally brought it to life (with the help of Claude). I was tossing up taking an actual Chromium fork and figuring out how to do the same thing with a proper, downloadable app, but Hell Fuck Nah.

You may know me from my previous petition to make Like Soda the Australian national anthem. You probably don't.

Incidentally, you can spot me as the shirtless man on stage at the Enmore from the Waco tour.

This was totally worth it. Hell Fuck Yeah.
